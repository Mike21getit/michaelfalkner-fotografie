About me

Images with attitude and feeling

I’m Michael Falkner, a photographer based in Königswinter near Bonn, Germany. My focus is portrait and fine art nude photography – images that show personality rather than perfection. I work with light and shadow, often in black and white or in warm, quiet tones.

Shoots take place in the Cologne/Bonn area – in the studio or on location. Beforehand we take our time to talk through your idea, style and boundaries, so that you feel comfortable in front of the camera. Respect, trust and discretion go without saying, especially for nude and boudoir sessions.
